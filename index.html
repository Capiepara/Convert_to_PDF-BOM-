Stage

    PDFs are redrawn from the Excel content using each sheet's print area, paper size and fit-to-page scale. Sharp vector keeps text, borders and fills as real vector graphics (crisp at any zoom, text can be selected). Fonts are replaced by Arial/Times/Courier look-alikes squeezed to the original Calibri widths; text with Chinese or other non-Western characters, and pictures, are embedded as images. Exact fonts (image) reproduces your own fonts but as a picture, so it gets blurry when zoomed. Hidden sheets are skipped.
